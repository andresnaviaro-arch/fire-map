# Template — Executive Package Outline (Weeks 11–12)

The capstone. Evidence first (sections 1–8), asks second (9–12). Build as a
deck or document; every claim traces to a portfolio artifact.

| # | Section | Content | Source artifact |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What agentic AI actually is | 2 slides, plain language, agency-as-spectrum | Week 1 concept map |
| 2 | Current organizational opportunity | Where AI creates value in our sales motion; the escalation ladder (not everything needs an agent) | Week 2 primer |
| 3 | Use-case prioritization | The scored matrix — shows method, not opinion | Scoring matrix |
| 4 | Selected workflow | Problem statement + "So what?" chain | Implementation brief §1, §3 |
| 5 | Current vs. future state | Two process maps side by side; what humans stop doing, keep doing | Implementation brief §2, §4 |
| 6 | Prototype demonstration | Live or recorded: one typical case + one graceful failure | Demo script |
| 7 | Risk and governance | Autonomy levels, owners, controls, kill switch | Governance charter |
| 8 | Business-value evidence | Baseline vs. shadow/pilot numbers; the eval improvement curve | Baseline table + eval report + shadow results |
| 9 | Pilot proposal | Scope, named users, duration, success criteria, cost, decision date | 90-day roadmap |
| 10 | Training plan | Awareness (90 min) / Practitioner (half day) / Builder (4 weeks) | Week 12 plan |
| 11 | AI capability roadmap | This workflow → adjacent workflows → team capability | Roadmap |
| 12 | Proposed role for you | Specific: pilot lead, trainer, use-case intake owner | — |

## The one-page ask

Separate single page: the pilot decision requested, by when, what it costs,
what success looks like, and your proposed role. Executives act on the one-pager;
the package is the supporting evidence.

## Pre-delivery checklist

- [ ] Demo run successfully three times the same morning
- [ ] One graceful failure shown on purpose
- [ ] The three killer questions answered *inside* the package:
  - "What happens when it's wrong?" → §7 + §8
  - "Why you?" → the portfolio itself
  - "What does this cost us?" → §9, including the cost of the status quo
- [ ] Every number traceable to an artifact
- [ ] Rehearsed against a skeptical listener
