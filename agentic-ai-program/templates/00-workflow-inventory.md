# First Assignment — Workflow Inventory

List **10–15 recurring workflows** from your job. Don't filter for "AI
suitability" yet — capture reality; the scoring in Week 4 does the filtering.
Copy this file to `work/workflow-inventory.md` and fill it in.

**Tips for a Wesco/Anixter AM covering the English Caribbean:** think across
quoting, follow-up, account planning, project/tender tracking, forecasting,
reporting, expediting, and internal coordination (pricing, PM, suppliers).
A workflow qualifies if it recurs, has a trigger, and eats real time.
