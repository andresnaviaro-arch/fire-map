# Weeks 11–12 — Becoming the Internal AI Trainer

**Goal:** convert the portfolio into two things: an executive package that wins
approval for the pilot, and the curriculum that positions you as the person who
trains the broader NOLA team.

The capstone serves two purposes at once:

1. Demonstrate that you can identify and implement a valuable AI workflow.
2. Establish the curriculum you could use to train the broader team.

## Week 11 — The executive package

Build the deck/document per `templates/05-executive-package-outline.md`. The
twelve sections:

1. **What agentic AI actually is** — 2 slides, from your Week 1 concept map; no hype
2. **Current organizational opportunity** — where AI produces value in our sales motion
3. **Use-case prioritization** — the scored matrix; shows method, not opinion
4. **Selected workflow** — the problem statement and "So what?" chain
5. **Current-state vs. future-state** — the two process maps side by side
6. **Prototype demonstration** — live or recorded; typical case + graceful failure
7. **Risk and governance model** — autonomy levels, owners, controls, kill switch
8. **Business-value evidence** — baseline vs. shadow/pilot numbers; the improvement curve
9. **Pilot proposal** — scope, users, duration, success criteria, cost, decision date
10. **Training plan** — see Week 12
11. **AI capability roadmap** — this workflow → adjacent workflows → team capability
12. **Proposed role and responsibilities for you** — specific: pilot lead, trainer, use-case intake

Sequencing matters: evidence (1–8) before asks (9–12). You are not requesting
permission to explore — you are presenting a working capability and proposing a
controlled way to scale it.

**The demo rules:** never demo anything you haven't run three times that
morning; always show one graceful failure on purpose — "watch what it does
when the data is missing" builds more trust than any success case.

## Week 12 — The training plan

Your curriculum for the team is this program, compressed. Three tiers:

| Tier | Audience | Duration | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Whole team | 90 min | What agents are/aren't, the demo, safe-use rules |
| Practitioner | AMs who will use the pilot | Half day | The user guide, approval workflow, when to override, how to report issues |
| Builder | 1–2 future co-builders | 4 weeks | Guided pass through Parts II–III of this program on a *second* workflow |

The Builder tier is the strategic one: the second workflow, built by someone
you trained with your templates, is the proof that this is a **repeatable
methodology** — a capability, not a one-person project.

## Final deliverables

- [ ] Executive package (deck or document, all 12 sections)
- [ ] 10-minute demo, rehearsed
- [ ] Training plan with the three tiers and dates you could run them
- [ ] One-page "ask": the pilot decision + your proposed role, on a single page

## Exit test

Deliver the package to a skeptical proxy (a colleague, or yourself recording
it). The three questions it must survive:

1. "What happens when it's wrong?" → governance model, evaluation numbers, kill switch
2. "Why you?" → the portfolio: nobody else in the region has built and evaluated one
3. "What does this cost us?" → run cost, your time, IT dependencies — and the cost of the baseline status quo

If all three answers are already *in the package* before they're asked, you're done.
