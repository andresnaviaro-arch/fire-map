# Your working folder

Copy templates from `../templates/` here as you complete each week, and keep
your filled-in versions in this folder. Keep the originals in `templates/`
clean — they are the reusable kit for the Builder tier of your training plan
(Week 12), when someone you train runs the methodology on a second workflow.

**Data hygiene rule:** nothing in this folder should contain unredacted
customer names, contact details, or confidential pricing if this repository
ever leaves your control. Redact before committing, or keep sensitive filled-in
versions in an approved company location and reference them here.

Suggested order:

1. `workflow-inventory.md` (first assignment — from `00-workflow-inventory.md`)
2. Week 3 audit artifacts (process map, baseline table, etc.)
3. `scoring-matrix.md`
4. `implementation-brief.md`
5. `governance-charter.md`
6. `evaluation-plan.md`
7. Executive package
